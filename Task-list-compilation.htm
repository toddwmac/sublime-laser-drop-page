Week 6 — The Art of Prospecting

Adding New Super Power

I know that was a firehose, but hopefully you got a sense of how this approach might be useful and how we might grab some incremental value.

Below are a few of the sample Lead Research AI Prompts that I showed you (Endotheia, Nissha, CenterPoint).

Tasks for This Week:

  • 1 Review and run the prompts, and see if the output was what you expected

    How would you change the prompt? (context, output request, formatting)

    How would you combine them?

  • 2 Run the same prompt through multiple models and compare the output

    Test across different chatbots and observe variations in response quality and style.

  • 3 Ask AI to build a prompt with just the context and your intention

    This is called meta-prompting, and it is a superpower. The example below adds some reasoning transparency and grounding. More on advanced prompting later.

    Example:

    I'm sales research assistant for Sublime Laser, a US contract manufacturer specializing in femtosecond (femto) and ultrafast laser micro-machining for medical devices — especially vascular & structural-heart work in Nitinol, precious metals, cobalt-chromium, titanium, stainless steel, and polymer tubes and components (ISO 13485).

    I am researching EndoTheia, Inc for potential sales opportunities.

    Using information from their website, and in particular their news posts (News | EndoTheia, Inc.) I want you to build me a prompt that I can submit to you or any other LLM, that will generate a rich set of sales research.

    Before you answer, outline your approach and confirm your sources.
